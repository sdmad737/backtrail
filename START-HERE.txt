laptop: follow docs/UI-START.md, then give prompts/01-ui.txt to your Codex.

   python3 -m http.server 4173 --directory explore --bind 127.0.0.1
   Open http://127.0.0.1:4173
2. On the prepared Mac, build the existing native Android app:
   source .tooling/env.sh
   ./gradlew --offline :app:assembleDebug :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:lintDebug
   A fresh clone needs its own JDK/Android SDK; see docs/UI-START.md.
3. Follow docs/SETUP.md on the Pixel for model transfer, full checksum
   verification, import and the physical offline acceptance flow.
