WHAT CHANGED
The reported short walk reproduced a real defect: a roughly2m GPS wiggle became
a bend/target despite roughly12m reported uncertainty. The new target policy
rejects that noise, follows sustained bends and uses local ordered route matching.
Three distinct fresh fixes acquire position. Passed intermediate waypoints advance
automatically without marking photos recognized, items collected or final arrival.
The route search stays local; crossings cannot jump to an arbitrary nearby section.
Weak/stale GPS, ambiguous matches, gaps and uncertain close directions pause arrows.
Numeric rotation-vector heading uncertainty over25degrees also suppresses guidance.

UI: full-screen map/camera; Map, Camera, Travis and Journeys bottom tabs; compact
current-journey status; explicit New versus Resume and editable journey names.
Capture: large shutter -> original-photo review -> optional note/reminder -> Save.
Retake/Cancel discard only the unsaved draft. Saving retains the original even if
the ViewModel is cleared while atomic metadata IO completes. Notes stay verbatim;
concise titles and local Gemma visual descriptions are editable. Explicit title
edits survive subsequent descriptions. Legacy generic journey names get dates.

GEMMA AND AR
Gemma describes saved photos, answers route-aware follow-up questions and compares
the current camera image with a saved landmark when requested. Geometry and sensors
drive the arrow and route progress. A visual model match never supplies coordinates,
steers the route or declares arrival. There is no continuous video-inference loop.
Camera direction is approximate, not a world-anchored AR object or visual SLAM.
