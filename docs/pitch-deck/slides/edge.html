Why the edge is the only honest place for this

Everything you will see today ran on the phone, in airplane mode.

No signal needed

The moment you need the way back is the moment there is no network. Every benchmark in this deck was run with airplane mode on and Wi-Fi off.

Nothing to leak

The app has no INTERNET permission at all (verified in the built APK). No account. Your trail, photos and notes never leave the device. One tap deletes a trip.

Open weights, your silicon

Gemma 4 E2B and EmbeddingGemma 2 run on the Tensor G6 NPU through LiteRT-LM. No API key, no per-call cost, no vendor in the loop.

This is the thesis slide: say it slowly. Judges have seen “on-device” claims that quietly fall back to the cloud. Ours cannot: the permission does not exist in the APK. Then the social-good line: an app like this is for everyone who walks, including people who cannot afford a subscription or a data plan abroad.
