The pipeline is prompting, schema-forced decoding, strict validators, EmbeddingGemma retrieval and a code-side safety guard. No weights were changed. Two rows go the other way, and we say so: stock Gemma abstained slightly more often, and reminder parsing is a wash.

Run on a Mac with the same LiteRT-LM 0.18.0 runtime and model files as the phone. Scripts, raw outputs and scoring rules: evals/RESULTS.md.

Three numbers to say out loud: zero to one hundred percent usable, four risky verdicts to zero, zero wrong same-spot calls in fifty-one. Then point at the two rows where stock wins. Judges remember the team that showed its losses; it makes the wins believable.
