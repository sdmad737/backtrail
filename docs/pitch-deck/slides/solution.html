What Travis does

Mark the spots on the way out. Travis finds them on the way back.

1

Save a place. One tap. Gemma 4 names the landmark and lists what will still be there: the sign, the gate, the pergola.

2

Say a note. “Pick up honey here on the way back.” Gemma turns it into a reminder that fires when you reach the spot.

3

Head back. Ask “where did I leave my water bottle?” EmbeddingGemma 2 finds the spot by meaning; the map and AR point along your recorded trail.

4

Check the spot. Point the camera. Gemma compares it with your saved photo and says “looks like the same spot” or, honestly, “not sure.” You confirm. Undo is always there.

Real app screens (Pixel 11 Pro build, airplane mode icon visible). The map is a bundled offline USGS tile pack for the venue area.

Four steps, thirty seconds. Point at the phone screenshots: this is the real app, not a mockup. The rule that runs through every step: the AI suggests, the walker decides. Nothing Gemma says moves you along the route by itself; your tap does, and Undo is one tap away.
