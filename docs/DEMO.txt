competing engines. The first integration follows all teammate commits on main.

offline Hybrid/Terrain/Aerial, numbered place previews, a scale bar and recenter.
Hybrid combines imagery and labels. Aerial uses higher-detail1024px exports at
close zoom. Map now includes a measured position cone and approximate true compass
bearing; display rotation is handled separately from the rear-camera AR bearing.

The logo's exterior is transparent and wordmark spacing is corrected.

BATTERY AND FEEDBACK
Camera runs only in the visible live AR view; capture uses CameraX while its
sheet is visible. One heading listener runs in visible Map/AR with a fresh GPS
fix and stops behind sheets, capture, replay or background. The decoded tile
cache is capped at24MiB; only visible tiles are retained for drawing.
Gemma runs on request, never every frame. GPS recording is independent.
Optional approach haptics require fresh fixes with <=15m reported accuracy,
three observations, meaningful closing beyond uncertainty, and a10-second
cooldown. They cue100/50/25m bands once per target; no stationary buzz.
Prepare contains the haptics switch. No phone battery-endurance claim is proven.
