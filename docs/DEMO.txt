BATTERY AND FEEDBACK
Camera and heading run only in the visible live AR view; capture uses CameraX
while its sheet is visible. Map rendering caches at most64 decoded tiles.
Gemma runs on request, never every frame. GPS recording is independent.
Optional approach haptics require fresh fixes with <=15m reported accuracy,
three observations, meaningful closing beyond uncertainty, and a10-second
cooldown. They cue100/50/25m bands once per target; no stationary buzz.
Prepare contains the haptics switch. No phone battery-endurance claim is proven.
